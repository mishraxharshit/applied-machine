# Code of Conduct

We want a friendly, useful community for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

**Expected:** be respectful, give specific and kind feedback, assume good intent, help newcomers.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may ban people who break these rules.

**Report problems** to the maintainers at the contact address in SECURITY.md (use the subject
"Conduct"). Reports are handled privately.

Inspired by the Contributor Covenant (contributor-covenant.org), which you may adopt in full later.
